About Us

A hundred years of one family's recipes

What began as a small sweet shop outside a Bombay mosque in 1926 has grown into a name recognised for its mithai, halwa and bakery specialities. The recipes have not changed — only the number of people who get to taste them.

Today, Abdul Gafoor Suleman Usman Mithaiwala is owned and operated from our Mumbai head office, with outlets running across India and the Gulf. Every item on our menu is still made to the family's original recipes for Aflatoon, halwa and Bombay bakery favourites.

1926 · Founder

Suleman Usman Mithaiwala

Started the family's sweet-making tradition in Bombay, setting down the recipes the brand still follows.

Second Generation

Abdul Gafoor Suleman Usman Mithaiwala

Carried his father's recipes and standards forward, building the shop into a trusted family name.

Today

Run from our Mumbai head office

The family tradition now runs as a growing brand, with a presence across India and the Gulf.

Our Presence

Seven outlets, three countries, one family recipe

From our first shop in Mumbai to counters across India and the Gulf, the Abdul Gafoor Suleman Usman Mithaiwala name now runs in seven locations across three countries — India, the UAE and Saudi Arabia — with more on the way through our franchise partners.
